# Research basis for BOSS V9 defaults

These sources inform the feature families and validation style; they do not prove that this implementation will be profitable on a given competition's universe, fees, or order book.

1. **Cakici, Shahzad, Będowska-Sójka & Zaremba (2024), “Machine learning and the cross-section of cryptocurrency returns,” International Review of Financial Analysis 94, 103244.** The study finds that simple characteristics such as past returns, price, illiquidity, and momentum are important, while incremental gains from model complexity are limited. This supports a regularized, interpretable ranking model rather than relying solely on a high-capacity classifier. BOSS also estimates covariance with exponential time-decay and shrinkage so the risk model adapts to regime changes without trusting a raw short-sample covariance matrix. [Article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1057521924001765)
2. **“Cross-sectional interactions in cryptocurrency returns” (International Review of Financial Analysis).** The study examines interactions among liquidity, risk, and past returns and notes that liquidity constraints and transaction costs can limit implementation. This supports explicit liquidity/spread features and a separate long/short ranker, but not blind inclusion of illiquid tokens. [Article](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1057521924007415)
3. **Pindza (2026), “Microstructure alpha: hierarchical learning and cross-asset transfer in cryptocurrency markets,” Frontiers in Blockchain.** The study finds that range-based spread proxies, realized volatility, and short-horizon momentum contain signal, but flexible models overfit under strict leakage controls and realistic fee assumptions can eliminate apparent profits. This informs conservative feature complexity, explicit slippage assumptions, and chronological/purged validation. [Article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2026.1811716/full)
4. **Roostoo API documentation.** The public repository describes API signing and short endpoints; verify permissions and the host supplied by the competition before live trading. [Official repository](https://github.com/roostoo/Roostoo-API-Documents)

## How to test changes after data collection

Use time-ordered walk-forward folds, with purge/embargo at least as long as the forward label horizon. Compare against simple baselines (market/asset momentum, equal-weight top/bottom decile, no-prediction ranker). Report gross and net returns, turnover, actual or estimated fees/slippage, long versus short P&L, drawdown, hit rate, rank IC, exposure, fill rate, and performance by liquidity bucket. Select parameters on earlier folds and report final metrics only on an untouched later period.
